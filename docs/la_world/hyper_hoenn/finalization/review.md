This includes the Frontier plazas and all 44 Frontier facility/side rooms, early-town interiors, native Trick House puzzles, ferry rooms, Artisan/Altering Cave and compatible event-island floors. All 123 registered Hoenn specials have structural entry and return paths. The audit checked 193 remaining construction scientists. In particular, donor Canalave/Outer Space/Champion Island aliases do not replace canonical Navel/Birth Island identities; Navel Rock crown scenes retain original assets because required native tile slots are absent from the donor bank.

# Portrait and overworld coverage

The 73 user labels are preserved in user-labels.json. The project has 42 registered overworld roster entries, including props; Giovanni and Cyrus also have separate Hyper sprites registered in Timeless Woods. The full donor overworld archive is separate from the active roster. A portrait label does not identify an overworld graphics ID.

- Match pending: 30
- Registered overworld: 33
- Alternate needs matching: 10

See roster-audit.csv for every labeled portrait. Pending rows may already have suitable art in the preserved donors or native graphics; they need a visual match before assignment. Alternate outfits and versions are tracked separately.

The gallery's Back Sprites view shows recovered full animation sheets. Hyper's Brendan and May have matching front/back palettes and corresponding silhouettes. The four additional Hyper back sheets retain tentative names pending review. FireRed-derived donors use slot labels because the originals may have been replaced. A back sprite can be reused for a player-side partner only after its character/variant is verified. No new back art is linked into the ROM by this gallery.

# Blue's Fallarbor and Mt. Chimney chapter

Blue must be briefed before the player leaves Fallarbor after Oak's introduction. The conversation uses only information the player has learned: Giovanni is named only after the Power Plant radio reveal. Completed Mt. Chimney saves bypass the new gate.

Blue meets the player at the lower cable-car station and boards after the attendant. At the summit he holds Aqua's checkpoint while the player tackles Magma's equipment operation. Both teams serve Giovanni's alliance. Blue rejoins the summit report, then stays with May and contacts Paradigm while the player follows Hoopa.

Cozmo's home and investigation conversations now explain the shared equipment and meteorite research. Existing rewards and Meteor Falls progression remain intact.

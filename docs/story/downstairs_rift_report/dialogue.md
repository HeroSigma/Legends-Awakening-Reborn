# Downstairs: the rift report

**ELARA:** {PLAYER}, come here a moment.  
You should see this.

*The player joins Elara at the TV.*

**NEWS ANCHOR:** We interrupt our scheduled Petalburg Gym report with a developing story.

**NEWS ANCHOR:** Unstable openings have appeared  
across Hoenn.  
  
Scientists are calling them rifts.

**NEWS ANCHOR:** Witnesses describe distant cities  
and unfamiliar mountains.  
  
These places do not match the  
surrounding landscape.

**NEWS ANCHOR:** The views vanish as the rifts close.  
  
Where they lead remains unknown.

**NEWS ANCHOR:** None of the rifts have stayed stable.  
  
Do not approach or enter them.

**{PLAYER}:** Places on the other side...?

**NEWS ANCHOR:** Unusual POKéMON outbreaks have been  
reported near the affected areas.

**NEWS ANCHOR:** Some of these POKéMON are native  
to regions far beyond Hoenn.  
  
How they arrived remains unknown.

**NEWS ANCHOR:** Scientists are investigating a link  
between the rifts and outbreaks.

**NEWS ANCHOR:** Lead scientist Colress is overseeing  
the investigation.  
  
Local researchers are assisting him.

**ELARA:** (quietly) Colress...?  
What is he doing here...?

**{PLAYER}:** You know him?

**ELARA:** I recognize the name.  
That's all.

**NEWS ANCHOR:** Report any unusual sightings to the  
research team.  
  
Keep a safe distance from all rifts.

**NEWS ANCHOR:** We will bring you further updates as information is confirmed.

*The broadcast ends.*

**{PLAYER}:** Rifts, strange POKéMON...  
Is that what you wanted to tell me?

**ELARA:** I wanted you to hear it before you went outside.

**ELARA:** Whatever those things are, they aren't somewhere to go exploring.

**{PLAYER}:** You don't have to look at me like that.

**ELARA:** Then don't give me a reason to.

**{PLAYER}:** What about the POKéMON?  
Are they all right?

**ELARA:** I hope so. Professor Birch has been looking into the sightings too.

**{PLAYER}:** Maybe he'll know what's going on.

**ELARA:** Try the house next door first. His family should know where he's working.

**{PLAYER}:** And Colress?

**ELARA:** Leave the scientists to their work.  
  
If you see a rift, keep your distance.

**{PLAYER}:** I'll be careful.

**ELARA:** That's not quite what I asked.

**ELARA:** Go on. And keep that package with you. We still don't know who sent it.

# Ashes and Echoes / Past Silph

The Woods departure points the player toward Cozmo, the Meteor Falls operation and Mt. Chimney. Aqua and Magma support Giovanni’s allied operation. After the summit, Hoopa offers a controlled crossing to past Saffron.

Use Kanto_SaffronCity and Kanto_SilphCo_1F through Kanto_SilphCo_11F (group 97), not the separate SilphCo_*_Frlg maps. These converted tilesets use the Emerald layout format.

Wally and shiny Kirlia are hiding beside the beds on Kanto_SilphCo_9F, at (4,16) and (4,17). Wally heals the party and stays on watch. Find him before confronting Petrel and Ariana on 11F at (0,17) and (2,17). Each admin has a distinct six-Pokémon singles team, legal level-capped members and healing items. Rocket guards are spread across floors 2–9.

On 11F, past and present Giovanni discuss their operation. Regular Mewtwo accompanies present Giovanni; an armored donor sprite has not been verified. Both Giovannis and Mewtwo escape through a rift. Hoopa rescues the player, Wally and Kirlia. Wally returns to his family; the player returns to Lavaridge and reports to Birch.

Hoopa is not a quest-item courier. Quest rewards go directly to the Bag. At the Silph rescue, a current level cap of at least 50 allows Hoopa to join immediately at level 50 (sent to storage if the party is full). Below that cap, Hoopa is an extra non-blocking overworld companion alongside the normal follower. It joins at the first eligible cap, currently 56 after the cap of 49. If party and storage are both full, it remains a companion and interaction retries the gift. Persistent flags prevent duplicate gifts.

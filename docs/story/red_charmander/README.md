# Red's familiar Kanto partner

Red always receives Charmander as his fresh partner, independently of the player's starter. Birch offers something familiar, explains that Charmander was found near a rift and brought to his lab by an aide, and asks Red to help it feel at home. Red responds: "Feels like I'm back in Kanto." Existing Birch/Red speaker tags and colors remain intact.

The early lab trainer and Route 103 partner data both use level-10 Charmander. The lab keeps its original physical build, Focus Sash and Aerial Ace, preserving the protected introductory spar. The partner retains Aerial Ace and replaces Riolu-only Quick Attack/Force Palm with Charmander's Ember and Smokescreen. Aerial Ace is in this project's Charmander teachable list; the current generation's level-up data grants Ember and Smokescreen before level 10. Pikachu's scene objects, following, rescue role and watching behavior are unchanged.

Native complete-chapter tests cover all three current starters for both protagonists and a compact 60-box save/continue. All 300 checks pass on the recorded ROM hash, including the actual lab loss, Charmander species/level, Route 103's four-trainer battle, rewards, money protection, party identity, progression flags, scene cleanup and subsequent save/continue. Tests use disposable batteries and never open the player save. The story tracer accepts the field renderer's inserted speaker-color prefix and uses the expanded inventory accessor.

This change updates Red and his dialogue; it does not expand the player's three currently selectable starter slots.
